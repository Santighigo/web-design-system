# 10 — References

Good sites are rarely invented from nothing. They combine a few specific ideas from great sites, applied to a brand's tokens.

## How to choose

- 3–5 references, not 20. More references produce mush.
- Prefer sites from adjacent fields with high craft (dev-tool and infrastructure landing pages are a great source of clean, systematic layouts).
- Name the one idea you take from each. Write it in the project `CLAUDE.md`.
- Treat moodboard images (retro-futuristic posters, HUD screens) as a *secondary flavor*, not something to reproduce literally.

## How to use

- Screenshot the exact part you're borrowing and keep it in the project.
- Before finalizing a component, put your screenshot next to the reference and compare proportions, spacing, shadow and radius.
- Take the pattern, not the brand: the layout idea, never their copy, logo, illustrations or colors.

## Patterns worth studying

Public sites that illustrate patterns from this system (study them, don't copy them):

| Pattern | Look for it on |
|---|---|
| Tabs connected to a panel, systematic sections | [mastra.ai](https://mastra.ai) |
| Isometric "system" diagram instead of a card grid | [arrakis.tech](https://www.arrakis.tech) |

Find your own for your brand. The point is the method.
